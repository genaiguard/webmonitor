Target price tracker with stock alerts

Set up a Target product monitor in two minutes. Get alerts on price drops, RedCard discounts, and stock changes.

Start free trialHow it works

WebMonitor.fyi tracks Target product pages and alerts you on price drops, restocks, and listing changes. The Target price tracker handles Cartwheel offers, RedCard promotions, weekly deals, and clearance pricing on whatever cadence you pick.

What it does

Cartwheel and weekly-deal detection

Target rotates promotions frequently. Catch the price change the moment it goes live, not when you remember to check.

Clearance threshold alerts

Set a price floor and get notified the moment a clearance item crosses it.

Stock and store-pickup tracking

Watch for restocks on out-of-stock items and pickup availability changes at your selected store.

Cross-retailer comparison

Run parallel monitors on Target, Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy for the same SKU.

How to set it up

1

Paste the Target product URL

Click Add New Monitor and drop in the product page link.

2

Write the criterion

Examples: "Alert me when this drops below $25." "Notify me on any Cartwheel discount." "Let me know when this is back in stock for pickup at my store."

3

Pick a frequency

Five-minute checks for limited-quantity deals; hourly for steady tracking.

4

Receive alerts

Email, SMS, or webhook — your choice per monitor.

Common use cases

  • Catch Cartwheel and weekly-deal pricing the moment it activates
  • Track Target clearance markdowns past a specific price floor
  • Monitor exclusive collections (Target collaborations) for restocks
  • Compare Target pricing to Amazon and Walmart on the same SKU
  • Watch a kids' product or seasonal item for back-to-school deals

Honest limits

WebMonitor reads the public Target product page. RedCard-only pricing visible after login, app-only Circle offers, and personalized prices are outside scope.

Frequently asked questions

Can I track Target Cartwheel discounts?

When the discount renders on the product page, yes. Cartwheel offers shown on the public page trigger an alert the same way any price change does.

Does this work for Target exclusive collections?

Yes. Many Target exclusives sell out in hours — a five-minute check interval with an SMS alert is the standard setup for restock tracking on these.

Can I monitor Target store pickup availability?

When the page surfaces pickup availability for your store, yes. Set the criterion to watch for "available for pickup".

Does Target block monitoring?

WebMonitor uses standard web requests with reasonable intervals. Target pages remain accessible at the cadences we offer. Aggressive sub-minute polling is not supported by design.
