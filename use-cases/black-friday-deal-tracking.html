Use case

Black Friday deal tracking

Set up monitors before Thanksgiving week, get alerts the moment a deal hits your threshold. Covers Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Costco — any retailer with a public product page.

Start free trialHow it works

Black Friday and the week leading into it produce more flash deals than any other shopping window of the year. Manual deal-watching means refreshing tabs and missing half the drops. WebMonitor.fyi runs the polling for you on five-minute intervals during the Black Friday window so the alert arrives within five minutes of the deal posting.

How to set it up

1

Build the wishlist before Thanksgiving

Start adding product URLs the week before. Trying to set up 20 monitors during the Friday morning rush is exactly when you don't want to be in the dashboard.

2

Set a target price per item

For each monitor, the criterion: "alert me when this drops below $X" or "notify me when this is at least 30% off". Specific thresholds beat "any change" — fewer false alerts.

3

Bump frequency to five minutes from Thanksgiving night

The dashboard lets you adjust per-monitor frequency. Bump high-priority items to five-minute intervals starting Thanksgiving evening through Cyber Monday midnight.

4

Route alerts to SMS for the high-stakes items

Email and Slack are fine for everyday tracking. For limited-stock items that sell out in minutes, SMS gets the alert in front of you fastest.

Who uses this

  • A laptop you've been waiting on — alert when it drops below $X
  • Limited-stock console restocks — five-minute checks, SMS alerts
  • Gift-list items with flexible criteria — "alert on any drop"
  • Coupon and discount-code activations on specific retailer pages
  • Tracking lightning-deal pages where products rotate every few hours

Honest limits

During peak Black Friday traffic, some retailers throttle product pages or briefly serve cached versions. Monitors are still reliable, but a 5-minute interval might effectively become a 6–8 minute interval if the retailer is rate-limiting. Plan accordingly for the highest-stakes items.

Frequently asked questions

When should I set up Black Friday monitors?

The week before Thanksgiving is the right window. Many retailers start dropping early Black Friday deals 10–14 days ahead, so monitors set up that early catch the early drops too.

Will the monitors keep working through Cyber Monday?

Yes. Monitors run continuously until you pause or delete them. Cyber Monday extends the deal window — many of the best electronics deals show up Sunday night into Monday.

Can I track competitor pricing during Black Friday too?

Yes — many e-commerce brands use Black Friday weekend specifically to watch competitor pricing for next-year planning. Set monitors on competitor pricing pages for the weekend.

What happens to my monitors after Cyber Monday?

They keep running until you change them. Many users bump the frequency back to hourly or daily after the weekend and leave the monitors as ongoing trackers for the next shopping cycle.
