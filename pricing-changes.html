How this works

Each product's pricing page is read once a day and its plans are listed as shown. A change is only recorded when two readings on consecutive days agree, so page glitches and short-lived tests are left out. Prices can vary by region, seat count and billing period; each product page links to its source. To track your own competitors this way, see competitor monitoring.
