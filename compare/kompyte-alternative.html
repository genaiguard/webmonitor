A Kompyte alternative with published pricing

Kompyte, now part of Semrush, automates competitive intelligence for sales and marketing teams on annual plans. WebMonitor is a smaller, self-serve option for teams who mainly need to know what competitors changed.

Start freeHow it works

Kompyte tracks competitor websites, content, ads, reviews and job postings, filters the results with AI and turns them into daily summaries, battlecards and win-loss reports. Its plans are sized for teams, starting at 10 competitors and 25 users, and are sold annually after a platform tour, without published prices. WebMonitor focuses on competitor websites, publishes its prices and bills monthly by card.

What you get with WebMonitor

Prices you can see

Starter is $29/mo for 25 pages checked every 6 hours, Growth is $79/mo for 100 pages checked hourly, and Agency is $149/mo for 250 pages.

Watchlists per market

Group competitors into a watchlist and WebMonitor finds their pricing, changelog, blog and careers pages automatically.

A weekly read, not a daily feed

One brief every Monday across all competitors in the watchlist, by email and Slack, with instant alerts only for the rules you set.

Built for a team of one

No seats to buy and no onboarding call. A founder or product marketer can set it up in an afternoon.

  • Want to see the price before talking to sales
  • Track fewer than ten competitors
  • Do not need ad, social or review tracking
  • Prefer monthly billing by card to an annual contract paid by invoice

When Kompyte is the better choice

Choose Kompyte if you need ad, social and review tracking alongside websites, battlecards and win-loss reports for a sales team, CRM and sales tool integrations, or if you already use Semrush. WebMonitor watches public web pages only and does not make battlecards.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Kompyte cost?

Kompyte does not publish prices on its plans page. Its tiers are Essentials (10 companies, 25 users), Professional (20 companies, 100 users) and Unlimited, sold annually. WebMonitor publishes its prices: $29, $79 or $149 a month.

Does WebMonitor track competitor ads and social posts?

No. WebMonitor watches public web pages such as pricing, changelogs, blogs and careers. Ads and social posts are not covered.

Does WebMonitor track competitor job postings?

Yes, through the competitor's careers page. The weekly brief notes new roles and what they suggest, such as a first sales hire in a new region.

Can I try it first?

Yes. One free competitor snapshot with no credit card, and paid plans are monthly.
