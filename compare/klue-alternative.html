A Klue alternative for teams without a CI budget

Klue is a full competitive intelligence platform for enablement teams. If you mainly need to know what your competitors changed each week, WebMonitor does that from $29 a month with no contract.

Start freeHow it works

Klue is built for companies with a competitive intelligence program: someone who curates intel, writes battlecards and runs win-loss interviews, with the output delivered into the CRM. It is sold through a demo and an annual contract, and buyers report paying around $30,000 a year. Most early-stage and small teams do not need that yet. They need to hear when a competitor changes pricing, ships a feature or starts hiring in a new market, without someone checking websites by hand. That is the part WebMonitor covers.

What you get with WebMonitor

The changes, without the program

Add competitors and WebMonitor finds their pricing, changelog, blog and careers pages, checks them on a schedule and tells you what changed. No analyst needed to keep it running.

A weekly brief your team reads

Every Monday each watchlist gets a short written summary of the week across all competitors in it, by email and in Slack.

Alerts on the changes that matter today

Add a plain-English rule to any page, such as "tell me if their Business plan price changes", and get an email as soon as it happens.

Monthly billing

Plans are $29, $79 or $149 a month and can be cancelled any time. When you outgrow it and hire a CI lead, you have a record of every competitor change to hand over.

  • Do not have a dedicated competitive intelligence person
  • Want competitor tracking running this week, not after a sales cycle
  • Need to know about pricing, product and hiring changes more than you need battlecards
  • Cannot justify a five-figure annual contract yet

When Klue is the better choice

Choose Klue if you have a CI or enablement team that will curate intel, need battlecards inside Salesforce and your sales tools, want win-loss interviews and analysis, or need intel from sources beyond public websites, such as calls, CRM notes and field reports. WebMonitor does not write battlecards, run win-loss programs or integrate with a CRM.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Klue cost?

Klue does not publish prices. Buyer data from Vendr puts most contracts between about $16,000 and $60,000 a year, with a median around $30,000. WebMonitor costs $348 to $1,788 a year on monthly billing.

Does WebMonitor make battlecards?

No. WebMonitor tells you what changed on competitor websites and summarises it each week. Many small teams paste the brief into their own battlecard doc, but there is no battlecard editor or CRM integration.

Can WebMonitor replace Klue?

For a team that mostly uses a CI tool to find out what competitors changed, yes. For a team that relies on battlecards in the CRM, win-loss analysis and curated intel from sales, no; Klue does much more.

Is there a free trial?

There is a free competitor snapshot instead of a trial. Paid plans are monthly, so you can cancel after the first month if it is not useful.
