A Visualping alternative built for watching competitors

Visualping is a strong page change detector. If what you really want is to know what your competitors changed this week, WebMonitor groups their pages into watchlists and writes you one brief every Monday.

Start freeHow it works

Visualping watches individual pages and tells you when each one changes, with an AI summary of the change. That works well for a handful of pages. Teams that use it for competitor tracking often end up with dozens of single-page monitors and a steady stream of alerts to read. WebMonitor starts from the competitor instead of the page: add a domain, it finds the pages worth watching, and the week's changes across all your competitors arrive as one written brief.

Why competitor-focused teams switch

Watchlists instead of one monitor per page

Enter a competitor's domain and WebMonitor reads its sitemap and homepage to suggest its pricing, changelog, blog, careers and integrations pages. Five competitors take a few minutes to set up, not an afternoon of copying URLs.

A brief you read once a week

Instead of an alert every time a page changes, you get a Monday summary of what each competitor changed and why it might matter. Quiet weeks are a single line.

Cosmetic changes left out

Each change is read by AI and only counted when the content means something: a new plan, a price, a feature, a role. Rotating banners and date stamps do not make it into the brief.

Slack without a business plan

Every paid plan can post the weekly brief and alerts to a Slack channel through a webhook, so sales and product see changes in the channel they already read.

Keep the suggested pages and add any Visualping URLs you want to carry over.

3

WebMonitor is the better fit if you

  • Track three or more competitors and care about what changed, not which pixels moved
  • Want one summary a week for your team instead of individual alerts
  • Need pricing, changelog and hiring changes from the same competitor in one place
  • Want Slack delivery on a plan under $100 a month

When Visualping is the better choice

Pick Visualping if you need visual screenshots and pixel diffs, checks every few minutes, monitoring of pages behind a login, or a free plan you can keep using. It also has years of polish as a general change detector, with a browser extension, an API and many integrations. WebMonitor is narrower on purpose: it reads text, focuses on competitors, and does not offer minute-level checks.

Frequently asked questions

Is WebMonitor cheaper than Visualping?

It depends on what you watch. Visualping Personal starts at $14 a month for 10 pages, which is cheaper for a few pages. For competitor tracking across 25 to 100 pages with Slack, WebMonitor costs $29 or $79 a month, while Visualping's Business plans with Slack start at $100 a month billed yearly.

Does WebMonitor take screenshots like Visualping?

No. WebMonitor reads the text of each page and describes the change in words. If you need to see visual layout changes, Visualping is the better tool.

Does Visualping have a weekly summary?

Visualping offers scheduled reports that bundle changes across monitors. WebMonitor's brief is written per watchlist, across competitors, and explains each change in plain sentences rather than listing page diffs.

Can I try WebMonitor for free?

Yes. One free competitor snapshot, no credit card. Watchlists and the weekly brief are on paid plans from $29 a month, billed monthly.
